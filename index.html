About

Hi, I'm Jordan.

I spent my twenties as a desk-bound engineer with a bad back. Learning to deadlift properly fixed it — and changed what I wanted to do with my life.

For the last ten years I've coached everyone from first-time gym-goers to masters powerlifters. My approach is simple: master the basics, progress a little every week, and build habits you can keep for decades.
